Connect your Google Sheet

This dashboard isn't connected to a sheet yet, so there's no data to show. It reads the CSV link Google Sheets gives you when you publish a tab to the web.

  1. In your Google Sheet, open File → Share → Publish to web.
  2. Pick the tab that holds your data, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), click Publish, and copy the link.
  3. Open this HTML file in a text editor, find CSV_URL near the top of the script, paste the link between the quotes, save, and reopen the page.
